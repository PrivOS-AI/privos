## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your thing works with.** "Compatible with PrivOS", "runs on
  PrivOS", "PrivOS integration", "consulting for PrivOS deployments". Use the
  mark in plain text, in the same typeface as the surrounding text, as an
  adjective describing our software, and make clear whose product yours is.
- **Refer to us in editorial and academic writing**, reviews, comparisons,
  talks, and course material.
- **Use the logo unmodified** to link to our website or repository, or to
  identify PrivOS in a comparison table, a slide, or an article, at a size and
  placement that does not suggest we are the source of your material.
- **Redistribute the software unmodified and free of charge under the name
  PrivOS**, on the conditions set out in Limitation 4 of the license. This is
  the one permission here that also depends on the license: if Limitation 4
  does not allow the distribution, this permission does not apply to it.

## What you need our written permission for

- Using "PrivOS" or a confusingly similar term **in the name of your product,
  service, company, app, or hosted offering** — including as part of a compound
  name such as "PrivOS Cloud", "PrivOSHost", "Priv-OS", or a domain such as
  privos-hosting.example. Note that offering the software under your own brand
  also requires a Partner License under Limitation 3; trademark permission
  alone is not enough.
- Using the marks on **merchandise**, or in advertising in a way that implies
  sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or certification.
- **Distributing a modified build under the PrivOS name.** Distributing a
  modified build at all requires a Partner License under Limitation 5 of the
  license; if you hold one, choose your own name for the result and describe it
  as "based on PrivOS" rather than as PrivOS.
- Using the marks in a **social media account name, handle, or avatar** that
  could be read as an official PrivOS account.

## What you may not do

- Register the marks, or a mark confusingly similar to them, as a trademark,
  domain name, or account name, for software or related goods and services.
- Alter the logo: no recoloring, stretching, rotating, adding elements, or
  combining it with your own mark.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests your product is our product, that we
  endorse or certify you, or that you are an official partner when you do not
  hold a Partner License.
- Use the marks in connection with unlawful content, or in a way that damages
  the reputation of PrivOS or Roxane, Inc.
- Remove or obscure the marks and notices from the software (Limitation 7 of
  the license).
